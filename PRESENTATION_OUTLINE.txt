Project Description
	Introduction: Transportation plays a significant role in economic and social development.
	The rapid growth of ICT has transformed traditional services into modern digital platforms.
	Challenges: Inefficient ride management, poor driver verification, and limited monitoring.
	Solution: STA RIDE integrates passengers, drivers, and admins into one centralized platform.
	Core Features: Secure authentication, real-time GPS tracking, and ride request management.

Development Methodology
	Agile Scrum: Supports iterative development, continuous testing, and regular feedback.
	Process: Planning -> Requirements Analysis -> System Design -> Development -> Testing -> Deployment.
	Technologies: Flutter, Dart, Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage).
	Tools & APIs: Google Maps API, Android Studio, Visual Studio Code.

Significance of the Project
	Passengers: Easy ride booking, real-time tracking, improved safety, and faster services.
	Drivers: Efficient ride management, driver verification, and earnings monitoring.
	Administrators: User management, driver verification, ride monitoring, and report generation.
	Organizations: Centralized administration, better operational efficiency, and improved decision making.

System Demonstration & Conclusion
	Modules: Login, User Registration, Dashboards (Rider, Driver, Admin).
	Demonstration: Ride Request, GPS Tracking, Driver Verification, and Reports.
