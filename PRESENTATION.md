# SLIDE 2: Project Description
**Introduction**
* Transportation is key to economic and social development.
* ICT has transformed traditional services into modern digital platforms.

**Problem Statement**
* Inefficient ride management.
* Poor driver verification.
* Limited administrative monitoring.
* Inadequate communication between passengers and drivers.

**The Solution: STA RIDE**
* Centralized platform for Passengers, Drivers, and Admins.
* Real-time GPS tracking and secure authentication.

**Agile Scrum Methodology**

# SLIDE 4: Significance of the Project
**Passengers**
